# PlayNbuildAnything — Naming Card

## Official spelling

**PlayNbuildAnything**

The spelling is intentional. **N** is the connector between building and play: Build **N** play anything.

## Meaning

PlayNbuildAnything is a global play-to-build SaaS: people play with ideas, learn by experimenting, collaborate with others and move toward real-world creation.

## Brand architecture

- **PlayNbuildAnything:** global parent platform and creation operating system.
- **Rasyne Deco:** founding atelier world and first production vertical.
- **Rasyne Worlds:** the first playable environment inside the Rasyne world.
- **Design Studio:** the first Build Deck experience for turning play into a structured brief.

## Usage rule

Use `PlayNbuildAnything` exactly as written in product strategy, platform documentation and future global identity work. Keep `Rasyne Deco` unchanged wherever the communication refers to the Sayada atelier, its services, its address or its craft work.
