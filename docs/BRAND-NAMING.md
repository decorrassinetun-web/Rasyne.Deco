# BuildNdoanyting — Naming Card

## Official spelling

**BuildNdoanyting**

The spelling is intentional. The **N** is read as **And**: Build **N** do anything.

## Meaning

BuildNdoanyting is a global play-to-build SaaS: people play with ideas, build understanding, collaborate with others and move toward real-world creation.

## Brand architecture

- **BuildNdoanyting:** global parent platform and creation operating system.
- **Rasyne Deco:** founding atelier world and first production vertical.
- **Rasyne Worlds:** the first playable environment inside the Rasyne world.
- **Design Studio:** the first Build Deck experience for turning play into a structured brief.

## Usage rule

Use `BuildNdoanyting` exactly as written in product strategy, platform documentation and future global identity work. Keep `Rasyne Deco` unchanged wherever the communication refers to the Sayada atelier, its services, its address or its craft work.
